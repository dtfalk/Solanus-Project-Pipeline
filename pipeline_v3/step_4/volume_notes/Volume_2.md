VOLUME-SPECIFIC NOTE (this page is from Volume_2 — mostly formal letters; the general rules apply, with these EMPHASES from the hand-review of this volume's sample pages):

1. src_origin vs src_location_sender — KEEP THEM STRAIGHT. src_origin is the INSTITUTION or place NAME only ('St. Michael's Monastery', 'Church of the Sacred Heart', 'SERAPHIC MASS ASSOCIATION'). src_location_sender is the ADDRESS — street and/or city/state lines ('225 Jerome Street, Brooklyn 7, N.Y.', 'Huntington, Indiana, Route 7'). The same split applies on the recipient side: src_recipient = the addressee NAME line; src_location_recipient = the address lines under it. Never fold an address into the name box or vice versa.

2. HEADER BOXES MUST BE TIGHT — this volume's recurring error is SPILL in the letterhead/address zone: the src_origin box swallowing the address line below it, the src_location_sender box reaching up into the institution name, recipient boxes grabbing the address under them. A small overlap of box EDGES is acceptable; what must be minimized is one box CONTAINING THE TEXT that belongs to the other box. In the header zone, draw each box snug around ITS OWN lines: top edge just above its first line of text, bottom edge just below its last line — do not extend a header box across the neighbouring role's line even when the lines sit close together.
