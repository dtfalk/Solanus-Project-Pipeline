# Cloning a Person's Voice and Mind in an LLM: Style + Stance Reproduction from a 2M-Word Unpaired Corpus

## TL;DR
- **Build a hybrid, not a pure pipeline or a pure single model.** The strongest concrete bet for a ~2M-word unpaired corpus + 250GB of GPU is a **single style-and-stance-tuned open model (QLoRA on a 70B, or full fine-tune of a 7–13B) trained on bootstrapped synthetic (neutral→styled) pairs plus a reconstruction/continuation objective, with RAG over the person's own writings to ground stances at inference.** The decoupled "style translator" (Approach 1) better preserves base-model reasoning but reliably loses fine-grained idiosyncrasy and almost never recovers genuine stance; the unified model (Approach 2) captures deep idiosyncrasy and opinions but risks catastrophic forgetting and inventing opinions — both failure modes are now individually addressable.
- **Stance faithfulness is the hard, under-solved problem and the place to ground in the corpus.** Surface style is largely solved by 2023–2026 methods (STRAP-style synthetic pairs, LUAR/StyleDistance embedding evaluators, activation steering). Faithful reproduction of *what the person actually believes* is not: models are sycophantic, drift off-persona, and confabulate opinions. The defensible design ties every stance to retrieved evidence from the corpus and *measures* opinion faithfulness, rather than trusting the fine-tune.
- **The neuroscience cross-pollination is real but partly speculative.** Horikawa's 2025 "mind captioning" (gradient-free, MLM-guided iterative optimization of text so its embedding matches a *decoded target representation*) is a genuine, transferable template: evolve text to match a target **style/stance embedding** (LUAR/StyleDistance/belief embedding) as the fitness function. No published NLP work yet applies this exact loop toward a style or belief embedding — that is a concrete, novel contribution opportunity. Encoding/decoding-model framing (Tang & Huth) and the Lee et al. 2025 belief-embedding space are best used as *evaluation/targeting* representations, not as decoders themselves.

## Key Findings

1. **Unpaired is the normal regime and it is well-served.** The canonical method is STRAP (Krishna, Wieting & Iyyer, EMNLP 2020): paraphrase the corpus into a "neutral" style to create pseudo-parallel pairs, then train an *inverse paraphraser* to rewrite neutral→target. Round-trip (back-)translation through a pivot language is an alternative neutralizer. With ~2M words you have far more than the low-resource setting (STYLL operated on 16 posts/author) and enough for robust supervised fine-tuning.

2. **Modern LLM prompting does style "okay" but not great, and fine-tuning wins.** Across systematic studies, zero/few-shot prompting of large models for TST does not surpass dedicated trained systems; parameter-efficient fine-tuning closes the gap to SOTA. So a frozen big model + clever prompt is a fast baseline but not the ceiling.

3. **Author-cloning by fine-tuning exists and works at modest scale.** Character-LLM (Shao et al., EMNLP 2023) fine-tunes 7B models on synthesized "experience" data to act as specific people; RoleLLM, CharacterGLM, Ditto, PersLLM extend this. These capture utterance style and some knowledge, but evaluations (digital-twin benchmarks, persona-consistency work) show they drift and don't reliably hold deep stance.

4. **Stance is separable, retrievable, and measurable — but easily faked.** Stance detection can be reframed as conditional generation grounded in external knowledge; the Lee, Aiyappa, Ahn, Kwak & An (2025, Nature Human Behaviour) belief-embedding space shows thousands of beliefs can be mapped into a fine-tuned-LLM embedding space capturing polarization/interrelation. Sycophancy (Sharma et al.) and persona drift are documented, well-quantified failure modes.

5. **Decoding-time steering is a powerful third axis.** Proxy-tuning / DExperts / logit arithmetic let a small tuned model steer a big *frozen* model; StyleVector (2025) shows personal writing style is a single activation-space direction that can steer generation training-free; Anthropic's persona vectors (2025) extract trait directions for monitoring and control. These keep base reasoning intact while injecting style/stance.

6. **The neuroscience methods are concrete enough to adapt.** Horikawa's optimizer is fully specified (below); style/belief embedding spaces give ready-made fitness functions. This is the clearest novel-contribution surface.

## Details

### 1. Text Style Transfer (TST): state of the art for the unpaired regime

**The core unpaired recipe — synthetic pseudo-parallel data.** Since the user has the person's corpus but no (neutral→styled) pairs, the dominant and most practical paradigm is to *manufacture* the pairs. **STRAP** (Krishna, Wieting & Iyyer, "Reformulating Unsupervised Style Transfer as Paraphrase Generation," EMNLP 2020; code at github.com/martiansideofthemoon/style-transfer-paraphrase) does this in three stages: (1) push the styled corpus through a *diverse paraphraser* to strip style and produce neutralized text; (2) train an *inverse paraphraser* to map neutral→original-style; (3) apply it at inference. Round-trip machine translation through a pivot language is an alternative style-neutralizer that preserves content while washing out stylistic features (Prabhumoye et al. 2018; a 2026 arXiv method, "Text Style Transfer with Parameter-efficient LLM Finetuning and Round-trip Translation," combines this with PEFT and RAG). A 2026 method ("Unsupervised Text Style Transfer for Controllable Intensity") refines synthetic-pair generation by using GPT-4o-mini to produce the styled side and a corpus-trained classifier to *filter* synthetic pairs that fail to hit the target style — a quality gate worth copying.

**Classical unpaired techniques** (useful as background and as auxiliary losses): disentanglement of content vs. style in latent space (Style Transformer, Dai et al. 2019, which notably *avoids* explicit disentanglement; John et al. 2019); denoising/reconstruction autoencoders; cycle-consistency / back-translation for style; reinforcement learning with style-classifier rewards; and diffusion paraphrasers (ParaGuide, 2023, plug-and-play guidance). The Jin et al. (2022) "Deep Learning for Text Style Transfer: A Survey" and the zhijing-jin/Text_Style_Transfer_Survey GitHub are the foundational reference points.

**LLM-based TST — how well it actually works.** Reif et al. (2022, "A recipe for arbitrary text style transfer with LLMs") established augmented zero-shot prompting. STYLL (Patel et al. 2023) extracts style descriptors from exemplars and few-shot prompts. But the systematic evaluation "Are Large Language Models Actually Good at Text Style Transfer?" (Mukherjee et al., 2024) finds GPT-3.5 and open LLMs show *promising but not SOTA* zero/few-shot performance, and that **parameter-efficient fine-tuning significantly improves results, aligning with SOTA**. Conclusion: prompting is a fast baseline; fine-tuning is the ceiling.

**Evaluation metrics (the standard triad).** (a) **Style strength/accuracy** — historically a style classifier; in the individual-author setting, authorship/style *embeddings* are used instead: **LUAR** (Universal Authorship Representation, Rivera-Soto et al. 2021), **Wegmann et al. (2022)** content-independent style embeddings, and **StyleDistance** (Patel et al. 2025, content-independent embeddings from synthetic parallel examples). Style accuracy = cosine movement toward the target author's embedding and away from the source. (b) **Content preservation** — BLEU/self-BLEU, BERTScore, Mutual Implication Score. (c) **Fluency** — perplexity, CoLA-classifier acceptability. ASTRAPOP (2024) uses LUAR directly as a reward model. A caution: LUAR conflates style and content, so StyleDistance/Wegmann embeddings are cleaner style-only judges (per the StyleDistance paper). A 2025 NAACL SRW paper ("Evaluating Text Style Transfer Evaluation: Are There Any Reliable Metrics?") flags that no single automatic metric is fully reliable — human evaluation remains necessary.

**Known failure modes:** style/content entanglement (style dilution when retrieval is content-driven); over-neutralization (losing meaning) or under-transfer (keeping source style); fluency collapse under strong steering; and metric gaming (a classifier-fooling output that humans don't find stylistically faithful).

### 2. Authorship / persona fine-tuning with LLMs

**What's published.** Character-LLM (Shao et al., EMNLP 2023; github.com/choosewhatulike/trainable-agents) trains LLaMA-7B agents via "Experience Reconstruction" — synthesizing detailed biographical scenes (profile, thinking, speaking) and fine-tuning with token-level cross-entropy, selecting checkpoints on interview dev sets. RoleLLM (Wang et al. 2023) shows injecting auto-annotated style data aligns LLMs to character language styles. CharacterGLM, Ditto (instruction-tuning), PersLLM (adds personality dynamics/subjectivity), and COMEDY (GPT-4-generated preference data + DPO) extend this. Digital-twin work is now a named subfield: TwinVoice (2025) benchmarks persona simulation across social/interpersonal/narrative dimensions; "How Far are LLMs from Being Our Digital Twins?" (ACL Findings 2025) introduces BehaviorChain (15,846 behaviors, 1,001 personas).

**Synthetic paired data as the bootstrap.** The recurring trick: use a big model to convert the unpaired corpus into supervised training signal. Variants: (i) STRAP-style neutral→styled pairs; (ii) "think-aloud"/verbalized-thought augmentation (arXiv:2510.09158) to model reasoning style; (iii) self-explanation distillation (Distilling TST with Self-Explanation, 2024) where the LLM emits CoT rationales for the rewrite; (iv) instruction-tuning on (prompt, person-styled response) pairs harvested by segmenting the corpus into prompt/continuation. PersonaHub (Chan et al. 2024) shows persona-conditioned synthetic data scales and improves diversity.

**Reconstruction/denoising on the unpaired corpus** is directly usable: continued pretraining / causal-LM fine-tuning on the raw 2M words instills lexical and rhythmic idiosyncrasy; denoising (mask-and-reconstruct, or neutralize-and-restore) teaches the model to *recover* the person's surface form.

**Model sizes & data volumes.** Character-LLM and most role-play work operate at 7B with tens of thousands of synthesized examples. With ~2M words you can: full-fine-tune a 7–13B model (feasible in 250GB), or QLoRA-tune a 70B (also feasible). ~2M words is ample for surface style (lexical, syntactic, rhythmic) and for a few hundred-to-thousand instruction pairs; it is *thin* for comprehensively covering the person's stances on arbitrary new topics — which is exactly why stance needs retrieval grounding, not just weights.

### 3. Faithful opinion / stance / value reproduction (the hard part)

**Why it's harder than style.** Style is a low-dimensional, pervasive signal present in every sentence; opinions are sparse, topic-contingent, and often *absent* from the corpus for any given new question. A model asked the person's view on a topic they never wrote about will *confabulate* — and RLHF-tuned base models are **sycophantic**, mirroring the user's stance rather than holding the person's (Sharma et al.; SycEval; the "BeliefShift" 2026 benchmark on temporal belief consistency). Persona-drift work shows models "drift in role when contextual cues invite stance shifts," producing semantic, style, and role drift; Abdulhai, Cheng, Clay, Althoff, Levine & Jaques ("Consistently Simulating Human Personas with Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning," NeurIPS 2025; arXiv:2511.00222) define prompt-to-line, line-to-line, and Q&A consistency metrics and, using them as reward signals for multi-turn RL across patient/student/social-chat roles, "reduce inconsistency by over 55%, resulting in more coherent, faithful, and trustworthy simulated users."

**Stance as conditional generation, grounded in knowledge.** Wen & Hauptmann (2023) reframed stance detection as conditional generation using Wikipedia knowledge — the template generalizes: condition generation on *retrieved stance evidence from the person's own writings*. StanceGen2024 / "Stance-Driven Multimodal Controlled Statement Generation" (arXiv:2504.03295) is recent stance-conditioned generation work.

**The belief-embedding space (key syllabus paper).** Lee, Aiyappa, Ahn, Kwak & An, "A semantic embedding space based on large language models for modelling human beliefs," *Nature Human Behaviour* 9(9):1928–1940 (2025); arXiv:2408.07237; code/data public. They fine-tune an LLM on online debate data to map thousands of beliefs into an embedding space that captures interconnectedness and polarization, and show positions in this space predict belief patterns. **Application here:** embed the person's stances (extracted from the corpus) as a point/region in such a space; use proximity in belief space both to *retrieve* the most relevant attested stance for a new question and to *measure* whether generated opinions are consistent with the person's known belief structure (vs. drifting to an opposing region).

**Preventing invented opinions.** The defensible architecture: (1) **RAG over the corpus** so any stance-bearing claim is anchored to retrieved passages the person actually wrote; (2) **abstention/hedging** when no attested stance exists (contrastive-decoding-with-abstention techniques exist); (3) **belief-space consistency checks** as a guardrail/reranker; (4) **persona/stance steering vectors** to hold position under sycophantic pressure. Anthropic's **persona vectors** (Chen et al., arXiv:2507.21509, 2025) extract trait directions from natural-language descriptions and can *monitor* drift during a conversation and *steer* against it — directly applicable to detecting when the clone is sliding off the person's stance.

### 4. Single model vs. pipeline vs. decoding-time steering

**Catastrophic forgetting / alignment tax.** Fine-tuning for style degrades general capability and can strip safety/reasoning (the alignment tax). LoRA generally forgets less than full fine-tuning when adapting to *dissimilar* domains (Biderman et al. 2024) but this is not universal (Shuttleworth et al. 2025); style adaptation is a relatively small domain shift, so forgetting is manageable but real. Mitigations: orthogonal-projection LoRA (OPLoRA), EWC-LoRA, data-replay (mixing in general instruction data), and self-distillation (SelfAug) to preserve the base distribution.

**The tradeoff, decided.**
- **Approach 1 (style translator):** Decoupling preserves the big model's reasoning/knowledge best, is modular (swap base models freely), and is the safest for capability. But two neutral→styled translators in series wash out exactly the fine-grained idiosyncrasy the user wants, the translator has *no access to the person's reasoning or stance* (it only restyles whatever the capable model already decided to say), and content/style entanglement causes style dilution. **It cannot reproduce opinions** — it only repaints them.
- **Approach 2 (unified model):** Best captures deep idiosyncrasy, first-person voice, and (via training data) stance and reasoning patterns; single artifact to serve. Costs: catastrophic forgetting, confabulated opinions, and drift. All three are now individually mitigable (replay/orthogonal LoRA; RAG grounding; persona-vector monitoring).
- **Decoding-time steering (third option):** **Proxy-tuning** (Liu et al., COLM 2024; "Tuning Language Models by Proxy," arXiv:2401.08565) tunes a small expert + uses the logit difference (expert − anti-expert) to steer a big *frozen* base. The authors report it "closes 91% of the performance gap between Llama2-13B and its directly tuned chat version, and 88% of the gap for the 70B model, when evaluated across knowledge, reasoning, and safety benchmarks." **DExperts** (Liu et al. 2021) is the product-of-experts ancestor. **StyleVector** (Zhang et al., arXiv:2503.05213, 2025) derives a per-user style direction by contrasting activations of authentic vs. style-agnostic responses and steers training-free, achieving "8% relative improvement in personalization quality while reducing storage requirements by 1700×" versus traditional PEFT (on LaMP/LongLaMP). **Activation steering / representation engineering** (Turner et al.; Zou et al.; CAA, Rimsky et al. 2024) and **persona vectors** generalize this — but steering vectors are documented to be *unreliable/inconsistent across behaviors* (Tan et al. 2024; "Understanding (Un)Reliability of Steering Vectors," 2025), so they are an add-on, not a foundation.

### 5. Cross-pollination from neuroscience

**(a) Horikawa "mind captioning" (Sci. Adv. 11(45):eadw1464, 2025) — the most transferable idea.** The method is a **gradient-free, MLM-guided iterative optimization** that evolves text so its semantic-feature representation matches a *target representation decoded from the brain*. Mechanism, precisely: start from a non-informative `<unk>` seed; each of 100 iterations runs three stages — **masking** (replace spans up to 3 words, or interpolate mask tokens anywhere including ends, recursively up to 2× to allow multi-mask growth; sample 5 masked candidates), **unmasking** (RoBERTa-large, vocab 50,265, samples 5 candidate fillers per mask), and **candidate selection** (score every candidate by the **Pearson correlation between its DeBERTa-large features and the target features, averaged across all layers**, with an exponential length penalty s = r·l^(−α), α=0.1; keep top 5). Search width 130/step; 5 independent restarts to avoid local optima. The system identified viewed videos with "close to fifty percent accuracy" from 100 candidates (chance = 1%), and for recalled (imagined) content "proficient subjects [achieved] nearly 40% accuracy in identifying recalled videos from 100 candidates," using 6 native-Japanese subjects (4 men, 2 women, ages 22–37) who viewed 2,180 silent clips. An *untrained* MLM (random suggestions, "analogous to a genetic algorithm") did **not** work — the pretrained MLM's contextual priors are essential. (Note: the optimization objective is layer-averaged feature *correlation* with a length penalty, **not** MSE or BERTScore; IDF-weighted BERTScore-P is used only to highlight tokens for visualization.)

**The transfer.** Replace "brain-decoded target features" with a **target style/stance embedding** derived from the person's corpus (their LUAR/StyleDistance centroid, or their region in a Lee-et-al. belief-embedding space), and use the *same* masking→unmasking→selection loop to **evolve a candidate response until its style/belief embedding matches the target** while a fluency/content term (and a base-LM likelihood term) keeps it coherent and on-topic. The subagent search confirmed **no published NLP paper applies this exact gradient-free MLM-search loop toward a style/authorship/belief embedding** — this is the clearest novel-contribution opportunity. The nearest pure-NLP analogues are Li et al., "Text Revision by On-the-Fly Representation Optimization" (AAAI 2022, arXiv:2204.07359 — iterative MLM span-replacement toward a target attribute/representation), "Locate&Edit" (arXiv:2407.00740, mask→MLM-candidates→energy-function selection), and Malmi et al., "Unsupervised TST with Padded MLMs" (EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2010.01054). Evolutionary/classifier-guided prompt optimization (EvoPrompt, PromptBreeder, EPO/Thompson et al. 2024 Pareto fluency-vs-activation) are the closely related families on the prompt side. The style-embedding-inversion cluster the syllabus names — "Interpreting Style Representations via Style-Eliciting Prompts" (arXiv:2606.05716), LISA (arXiv:2305.12696), TinyStyler (arXiv:2406.15586) — decodes/inverts style embeddings via *trained decoders or LLM prompting*, not gradient-free iterative refinement, reinforcing the gap.

**(b) Encoding/decoding models & representation alignment (Tang & Huth, "Semantic reconstruction of continuous language from non-invasive brain recordings," Nat. Neurosci. 26:858–866, 2023).** Tang & Huth decode *toward a semantic target* by scoring candidate continuations against predicted brain representations — conceptually the same "generate-and-rank against a target representation" loop. For this project, the transferable principle is **representation-target decoding**: train a linear (ridge) map between the person's stance/style and an LLM feature space, then generate toward that target. Encoding-model thinking also motivates aligning the *generator's internal representations* to a target style/belief subspace (i.e., representation engineering with a corpus-derived target).

**(c) Belief-embedding space (Lee et al. 2025)** — covered above; best used as the **targeting and evaluation** representation for stance, not as a generator.

**Speculative vs. grounded.** Grounded today: synthetic-pair SFT, RAG grounding, activation/persona steering, proxy-tuning, LUAR/StyleDistance evaluation. Genuinely novel/speculative (the research frontier): (i) Horikawa-style **iterative evolution of text toward a corpus-derived style+belief embedding** as a decoding-time refiner; (ii) using the **belief-embedding space as a steering target and a faithfulness metric** simultaneously; (iii) joint optimization of a *style* embedding target and a *belief* embedding target with a fluency/likelihood regularizer.

## Recommendations

**Stage 0 — Data preparation (do regardless of architecture).**
- Segment the 2M words into (a) raw documents for causal-LM continued-pretraining; (b) prompt/continuation pairs (use headings, questions, topic sentences as prompts); (c) a **stance/exemplar retrieval index** (chunk + embed every passage; tag stance-bearing passages).
- Bootstrap **synthetic (neutral→styled) pairs** via STRAP (diverse paraphrase to neutralize) and/or round-trip translation; **filter** pairs with a corpus-trained style classifier and a LUAR/StyleDistance similarity gate (copy the 2026 controllable-intensity filtering step).
- Build the **person's style centroid** (LUAR + StyleDistance) and, if pursuing stance, embed their attested positions in a belief space (Lee et al. method or a fine-tuned stance encoder).

**Stage 1 — Baseline (days).** Few-shot prompt a strong frozen model (the Approach-1 translator, or restyle-in-place) to set a floor on style accuracy/content/fluency and stance faithfulness. Expect mediocre idiosyncrasy and poor stance — this calibrates the gap.

**Stage 2 — Recommended core build (the hybrid).**
1. **Generator:** QLoRA-fine-tune a 70B (or full-fine-tune a 7–13B) on: raw-corpus causal LM (rhythm/lexicon) + synthetic neutral→styled pairs (controllable restyling) + instruction pairs (first-person voice). Mix in general instruction data / use orthogonal-LoRA or replay to limit forgetting.
2. **Grounding:** wrap with **RAG over the corpus**; require stance-bearing outputs to cite retrieved passages; **abstain/hedge** when no attested stance is retrieved. Add a **belief-space consistency reranker**.
3. **Control:** add **persona/stance steering vectors** (StyleVector-style direction + Anthropic-persona-vector monitoring) to hold voice and resist sycophantic drift; keep magnitude modest given steering-vector unreliability.

**Stage 3 — If capability retention is paramount,** keep the big model frozen and use **proxy-tuning**: tune a small 7B expert on the person's data, steer the frozen 70B at decode time (expert − anti-expert logit offset). This preserves reasoning best and is the cleanest "decouple capability from style/stance" path — strictly better than the two-translator pipeline because the small expert *does* encode the person's distribution.

**Stage 4 — Novel-contribution layer (research upside).** Add a Horikawa-style **iterative refinement decoder**: generate a draft, then evolve it with masked-LM edits scored by similarity to the person's **style embedding and belief-space target** (plus a base-LM fluency term and a length penalty). This is the unexplored gap; it can sharpen fine-grained idiosyncrasy and pull stance toward the attested region as a final polish.

**Evaluation (build this before scaling training).**
- **Fine-grained style:** LUAR + StyleDistance + Wegmann cosine to the target author; **authorship-attribution classifier as a discriminator** (can a trained AA model tell clone output from real?); stylometric features (function-word distributions, sentence-length/rhythm, punctuation, type-token ratio); perplexity under a corpus-trained LM.
- **Stance faithfulness:** held-out opinion QA where ground truth is the person's attested position; belief-space distance between generated stance and attested stance; a **sycophancy stress test** (push opposing views, measure drift); abstention accuracy on topics the person never addressed (penalize confabulation).
- **Capability retention:** standard reasoning/knowledge benchmarks before/after, to quantify the alignment tax.
- **Human evaluation:** expert raters (ideally people who know the person's writing) judge voice authenticity and opinion faithfulness — necessary because no automatic metric is fully reliable.

**Thresholds that change the plan:** if the AA-discriminator can trivially separate outputs → strengthen synthetic-pair SFT and add the iterative refiner. If reasoning benchmarks drop sharply → move from unified fine-tune to proxy-tuning/steering. If confabulation/sycophancy is high → tighten RAG grounding + abstention and increase persona-vector steering.

**Tools/codebases:** STRAP (martiansideofthemoon/style-transfer-paraphrase); proxy-tuning (alisawuffles/proxy-tuning); LUAR, StyleDistance, Wegmann style embeddings; Lee et al. belief-space code; Anthropic persona-vectors; Horikawa MindCaptioning (horikawa-t/MindCaptioning); ParaGuide; CAA/RepE steering repos; PEFT/QLoRA (HuggingFace).

## Caveats
- **Ethics/consent:** cloning a real person's voice *and* opinions raises impersonation, consent, and misinformation risks; persona/belief steering can be misused. Tang & Huth themselves stress mental-privacy safeguards. Treat consent and disclosure as gating requirements.
- **Confabulated stance is the central technical risk:** weights alone will invent opinions; only retrieval grounding + abstention + faithfulness metrics keep this honest. Do not trust a fine-tune to "know" the person's view on unseen topics.
- **Steering-vector reliability is uneven** across behaviors — use steering as an adjunct, validate per-trait, don't make it the backbone.
- **Metric gaming:** LUAR conflates style and content; classifier-fooling outputs may not read as authentic to humans. Always pair automatic metrics with human evaluation.
- **The neuroscience analogy has limits:** brain decoding optimizes toward a *measured* neural target; here the "target" is a corpus-derived embedding that is itself a lossy proxy for style/belief. The iterative-refinement idea is promising but unproven for this use case — pilot before committing.
- **Recency flags:** several cited works are very recent arXiv preprints (some 2026-dated, e.g., the round-trip-translation TST paper arXiv:2602.15013 and the style-eliciting-prompts paper arXiv:2606.05716) and not yet peer-reviewed; treat their specific numbers as provisional.
